Interferometer Controls

Each photon travels both arms of the interferometer at once. The phase shift e^iφ in the upper arm sets how it interferes with itself when the arms recombine — that interference pattern is what a phase estimation circuit is built to read out.

Mach–Zehnder Interferometer

A single photon enters, splits across two arms, picks up a phase shift e^iφ in the upper arm, and recombines. Detector 0 and Detector 1 click with probabilities set entirely by θ.
